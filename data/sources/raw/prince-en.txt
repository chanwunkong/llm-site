# 沙漠裡的飛行員

A pilot flies a small airplane over the desert.

The airplane breaks, and the pilot falls into the sand.

He is alone, and he has little water.

In the morning, a small voice wakes him.

A little prince stands in the sand.

The prince asks the pilot to draw a sheep.

The pilot draws a sheep, but the prince does not like it.

The second sheep is too old.

Then the pilot draws a box.

The pilot says that the sheep sleeps in the box.

The prince looks at the box and smiles.

The prince comes from a very small planet.

His planet is as small as a house.

# 小小的星球

Every morning, the prince cleans his small planet.

He cleans three small volcanoes.

He pulls young baobabs from the ground.

Big baobabs can break a small planet.

A sheep eats small trees, so the prince wants a sheep.

The prince likes the sunset very much.

When he is sad, he watches the sunset.

The sun goes down, and the sky turns red.

The prince sits alone and looks at the stars.

# 玫瑰

One day, a new flower grows on the planet.

The flower is a beautiful red rose.

The rose is proud, and she asks for water.

The prince gives the rose fresh water every morning.

At night, he puts a glass cover on the rose.

The rose says that the wind is cold.

The prince loves the rose, but he does not understand her.

He feels sad, and he leaves his planet.

On the last morning, the rose says that she loves him.

The prince flies away with the birds.

# 狐狸

On the earth, the prince meets a fox.

The fox asks the prince to tame him.

The prince comes every day and sits a little closer.

The fox waits for the prince at the same time.

Now the fox is happy when he hears the prince.

The prince learns that his rose is special.

The fox tells him a secret about the heart.

Important things are not visible to the eyes.
